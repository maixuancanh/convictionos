# Demo script

1. Open the trust-first landing page. Show the product promise: AI options decisions with deterministic risk authority.
2. Click "Open Mission Control" and enter the dashboard.
3. Show readiness: paper-only mode, release SHA, broker connection status, scheduler status, and limitations.
4. Open the strategy panel. Explain catalyst, swing, and thematic mandates plus their separate risk budgets.
5. Show an abstention decision. Emphasize that no trade is a valid agent output when evidence or risk is insufficient.
6. If a verified paper lifecycle exists, show the paper order, reconciliation, lifecycle state, and receipt. If not, show the guarded acceptance report and say the project does not fabricate P&L.
7. Show runtime controls: pause, closing-only, frozen review, incidents, and recovery evidence.
8. Show receipt verification: what evidence was used, what AI concluded, what deterministic gates allowed or denied.
9. Close with the core claim: ConvictionOS is built to make AI trading accountable enough to become a serious SaaS product.
