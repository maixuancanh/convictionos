# Business value

ConvictionOS is positioned as a trust layer for AI trading automation. Instead of selling vague alpha, it sells explainability, paper-risk discipline, and operational proof.

Retail users get a safer way to learn options automation: every decision is labeled, bounded, and reviewable. Professional users get infrastructure patterns they already expect: role separation, audit receipts, incident controls, deployment health, and reproducible claim evidence.

Commercial expansion paths include premium strategy tournaments, private portfolio workspaces, broker/account integrations, paper-to-review workflows, receipt verification, and managed model/provider policies. The near-term moat is not one secret signal; it is the combination of AI reasoning, deterministic risk authority, broker reconciliation, and SaaS-grade trust UX.
