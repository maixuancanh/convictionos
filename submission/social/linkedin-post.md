I built ConvictionOS for the Alpaca AI Trading Agents Hackathon.

ConvictionOS is a paper-only autonomous options trading agent designed as a commercial trust-first SaaS product. The core idea is simple: AI can reason about market evidence, but deterministic systems must own execution authority.

The platform includes grounded AI analysis, catalyst/swing/thematic options mandates, risk gates, Alpaca paper trading integration, broker reconciliation, runtime controls, health checks, incidents, and decision receipts.

It does not promise returns. It focuses on making AI trading auditable, safer to evaluate, and commercially understandable.
