How ConvictionOS works

From evidence to an auditable decision

One controlled path separates model reasoning from trading authority.

01

Ground the thesis

Point-in-time market evidence, volatility context, and quant signals become a structured thesis—not a free-form order.

02

Enforce the mandate

Deterministic gates check liquidity, quote age, exposure, buying power, structure, and lifecycle policy.

03

Prove the outcome

Submission, rejection, abstention, reconciliation, and exit events produce a clear decision receipt.

Strategy lifecycle

Conviction is earned in stages.

Research never becomes paper capital because a model sounded confident.

  1. 1Drafted

    Thesis, universe, horizon, and risk rules are explicit.

  2. 2Validated

    Inputs, calculations, and refusal behavior pass checks.

  3. 3Backtested

    Evidence is evaluated without rewriting history.

  4. 4Published

    A versioned mandate is approved for observation.

  5. 5Paper running

    Only eligible strategies receive bounded paper capital.

Evidence standards

Performance without performance theater

We separate system readiness, paper execution, and strategy evidence. A working agent is not automatically a proven edge—and the interface never pretends otherwise.

SystemCan it operate safely?

Runtime, data capability, broker connection, and refusal paths.

ExecutionWhat happened on paper?

Orders, fills, lifecycle events, and reconciliation outcomes.

ResearchHas the strategy earned promotion?

Versioned tests, costs, calibration, drawdown, and sample quality.
